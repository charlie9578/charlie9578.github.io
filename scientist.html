Wind, data and open work

From blades to bytes.

I started with the question of how to control a wind turbine. Since then I’ve worked across renewable asset performance, operational data and software. The thread is the same: understand the system, make the evidence useful, and share what can be shared.

Four threads

Research, practical analysis and open collaboration have all shaped how I think about renewable energy.

01 / Research

Smart rotor control

My PhD studied trailing-edge flaps and wind turbine control: ways to reduce loads while keeping a turbine productive. That work led to papers on control strategies and fault response.

02 / Open data

Real wind farms, shared openly

I helped release operational data from Kelmarsh and Penmanshiel so more people can test methods, ask questions and learn from real turbines.
